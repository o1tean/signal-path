Playground

Poke at the physics

Every simulator here is also embedded in the module that teaches it. This page collects them so you can come back and play without re-reading.

Flipper Screen

Drive the Sub-GHz menus with a D-pad. Learn the menu tree before you touch the real device.

Spectrum Scope

A simulated spectrum analyzer with a live noise floor. Press a "remote" and watch its peak appear.

Tune In

A mystery device transmits. Read the peak and name the frequency. Five in a row earns the Tuned In badge.

Wave Lab

Type bits, choose OOK or FSK, and watch the data ride the carrier.

RF Calculator

Wavelength and antenna length from frequency; dBm to milliwatts and back.

Pulse Decoder

Turn a pulse train into bits the way the Flipper's Read mode does.

Rolling Code Sim

A remote, a receiver with an acceptance window, and a Flipper that captures and replays. See exactly why a copy works once, then stops.

Tool Picker

Given a scenario, pick the right Flipper tool: Read, Read RAW, Frequency Analyzer, or "stop — that's rolling code."

Flashcards

The glossary as flip cards. Space to flip, arrows to move.

Signal Atlas

Log the RF devices you own: frequency, modulation, protocol, fixed or rolling. Saved in this browser; export as JSON or CSV.

Signal Path teaches RF with devices you own, for learning and legitimate testing. Radio transmission is regulated where you live; interfering with other people's devices is illegal and harmful. When in doubt: listen, don't transmit.

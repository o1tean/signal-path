## Voice
Calm, exact, a little dry. A senior engineer who teaches well. Short sentences, concrete nouns, numbers where they exist. Second person. British/EU spelling is fine; be consistent per page.

## Never write
- Contrast reveals: "It's not X, it's Y." / "Not just X, but Y."
- Negation lists: "No fluff, no forks, no fear."
- Trailing pile-ons after a complete claim.
- Self-answered questions and colon reveals: "The result? …" / "The best part: …"
- Stock openers: "In today's…", "Whether you're…", "Say goodbye to…", "Unlock…", "X, reimagined".
- Em dashes in headlines, subheads, buttons, nav, meta descriptions. Avoid them in body copy too.
- Exclamation marks. Buzzwords (seamless, powerful, robust, streamline, elevate, journey, master in the hero).
- Any claim we cannot back: no "hack", no "bypass", no certification, no earnings, no "legal everywhere".

## Keep rare
One fragment and one list of three per section. Swap test: if a line would work on another course's site, rewrite it with a Signal Path fact.

## Page jobs
- Home: one promise, show the tools, show the path, show the price, one CTA ("Start the free starter" / "Get Signal Path Complete").
- Course: the six stages, what you can do after each, which lessons are free.
- Pricing: two tiers, the bonus stack with values, the guarantee, FAQ that answers legality, hardware needed, time, refunds, updates, VAT/invoice.

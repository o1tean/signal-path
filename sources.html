Corrections in this edition

  • Owning equipment and an “educational” label do not establish legal permission; receiving can involve protected data.
  • Official firmware is a suitable starting point. Custom presets and external CC1101 support are not exclusive to Momentum.
  • A consumed rolling code does not become fresh just because a forward window exists. Operational remote replay labs have been replaced with simulation.
  • The RF atlas is a foundation milestone. The course now continues through other interfaces and a reproducible portfolio.
  • FM238/FM476 refer to frequency deviation, not 238/476 kHz receiver bandwidth.

Playground

Ten interactive tools to learn with

  • Wave Lab — type a bit pattern, watch it ride a carrier as OOK or FSK.
  • Spectrum Scope & Tune In — a live scope with a noise floor; press a "remote" and name its frequency.
  • Rolling Code Sim — a remote, a receiver, a counter window. Capture, replay, watch it fail.
  • Pulse Decoder — turn a pulse train into bits, Princeton-style.
  • Flipper Screen — navigate the Sub-GHz menus with a D-pad before you touch the real one.
  • Flashcards, RF Calculator, Tool Picker, Signal Atlas.

The one rule Only capture and replay signals from devices you own or have explicit permission to test, in a space you control. Reception can involve private data; transmission must satisfy Romanian radio conditions. Never transmit to cars, gates, or garages that aren't yours, never jam or flood a band, and keep regional restrictions enabled for the course. Full ground rules →

Signal Path teaches RF with devices you own, for learning and legitimate testing. Radio transmission is regulated where you live; interfering with other people's devices is illegal and harmful. When scope or legality is unclear, use the simulators.

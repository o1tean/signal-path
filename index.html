The one rule Only capture and replay signals from devices you own or have explicit permission to test, in a space you control. Receiving is passive; transmitting is regulated and can interfere with other people. Never transmit to cars, gates, or garages that aren't yours, never jam or flood a band, and treat Momentum's region unlocks as "know your local law first." Full ground rules →

Signal Path teaches RF with devices you own, for learning and legitimate testing. Radio transmission is regulated where you live; interfering with other people's devices is illegal and harmful. When in doubt: listen, don't transmit.
